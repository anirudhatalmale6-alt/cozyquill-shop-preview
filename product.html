The Story

Ready to fall for someone you haven't met yet?

Blind Date With a Book takes the cover out of the equation and lets the story do the flirting. Choose your romance category, tell us a little about your reading tastes, and The Cozy Quill will hand-pick a mystery book for you.

Your book arrives wrapped, with only a few tantalising clues between you and your next fictional obsession.

You won't know the title. You won't know the author. You'll just have to trust the chemistry.

Because sometimes the best love stories start with a blind date. 💌
