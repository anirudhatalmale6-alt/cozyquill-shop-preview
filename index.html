Seven Ways to Fall

Every category is its own stock line, so when one sells out the others carry on. You'll manage these counts yourself from the admin area.

Cozy Escape

Soft, comforting romance for when you want warm feelings, gentle chemistry and somewhere lovely to disappear.

Sports Romance

Competitive chemistry, sporting rivalries and athletes who are considerably better at their sport than communicating their feelings.

Bookish Romance

Bookshops, writers, readers and literary love stories made for people who already consider fictional characters a legitimate relationship category.

Rom-Com

Banter, chaos, ridiculous situations and enough chemistry to make embarrassing yourself completely worthwhile.

Heartwarming

Tender, emotional romance with big feelings, genuine connection and the kind of ending that leaves your heart pleasantly squished.

Fantasy Romance

Magic, danger, impossible worlds and people falling inconveniently in love while considerably larger problems are occurring.

Morally Grey

Questionable decisions. Dangerous attraction. Characters who absolutely should come with warning labels and somehow make you turn another page anyway.
